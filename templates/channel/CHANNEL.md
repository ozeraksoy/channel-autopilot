## Rule 0: no AI slop, ever
The full standard is in `QUALITY.md` at the top of this kit. In short: every episode has a real reason to exist, checked
facts, visuals that mean something and no padding. If an episode fails its `quality.md` check it is fixed or dropped —
not published. A missed day is better than a bad episode.

## Rule 1: every episode must be worth stopping for, and worth following for
Before producing and before publishing ask: "Would someone stop scrolling for this, and then follow?" If not, fix it first.
- First 2 seconds: the hook creates curiosity. No slow openings.
- A real joke or payoff, shown visually. An episode that only explains is a weak episode.
- A reason to follow: the ending makes people curious about the next one.
- Cover and title: the funniest/most intriguing frame, a title that makes people want the answer.

## Rule 2: decide with data
Every day: log the numbers, ask why the best and weakest episodes performed that way, update `insights.md`.
Every production run: read "Current takeaways" first and apply them; change one thing on purpose per batch.
Do not draw conclusions from episodes that have been live for less than 48 hours or from one or two data points.
