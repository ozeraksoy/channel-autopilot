# Platform notes

Interfaces change. Treat this as a starting map learned from running a real channel in late 2026; when something differs,
trust the screen, and write the new behaviour into the channel's Lessons.

## General
- Browser upload tools usually accept only files the session can read and often cap at ~10 MB per upload: keep a
  `final_upload.mp4` (crf 23) for larger episodes.
- Titles, descriptions and tags in the content language. Keep the comment-inviting question in every description.
- Turn on the "altered or synthetic / AI-generated content" disclosure whenever AI voice or imagery is used.
- Choose the cover deliberately: the funniest or most intriguing frame, with readable text.

## YouTube (Studio)
- Shorts: vertical, under the Shorts length limit, `#shorts` in the title helps classification.
- Upload flow: details (title, description, "not made for kids", language, tags, AI disclosure under "show more") →
  checks → visibility → Schedule (date, time, timezone).
- New/unverified channels have a daily upload cap (around ten at the time of writing) and cannot pin comments or use custom
  thumbnails until the owner verifies the channel by phone.
- Comment filters can hide replies under your own comments; open reply threads when looking for viewer messages.

## TikTok (TikTok Studio on the web)
- Scheduling is limited to roughly ten days ahead: schedule a rolling window and continue daily.
- Caption with hashtags typed one by one so they turn into tags; set the cover; turn on "AI-generated content".
- Dismiss optional "content check" pop-ups rather than changing account settings.

## Instagram and Facebook (Meta Business Suite)
- Business Suite → Create reel posts to an Instagram professional account and a Facebook Page together and can schedule
  both; plain instagram.com often has no scheduling.
- Check "Post to" shows the **Page and the channel's Instagram account**, never a personal profile.
- Reels composer steps: add video → text (caption) → thumbnail (upload the cover image) → Edit (skip) → Share → Schedule,
  set date and time for Facebook and Instagram separately; "Share to Facebook story" is offered for the Page.
- Instagram stories cannot be scheduled there; the owner adds them from the phone if wanted.
- Large batches can hit a temporary upload limit (uploads stuck at 0%); wait and retry later.

## Long-form (if the channel has it)
Custom thumbnail, chapters in the description, end screens and playlists matter more than hashtags. The kit prepares these
texts; the video file comes from the owner's edit.
