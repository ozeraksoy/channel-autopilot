# Engagement

Early comments are the cheapest growth a small channel has: a reply turns a passer-by into a follower, and requests are free
market research. Replying publicly is posting on the owner's behalf, so do it only under the standing permission in
`CHANNEL.md`; without one, draft the replies and let the owner approve them.

## Daily round (after the day's episode is live)
1. **Channel comment** on today's episode on each platform: the standing question from `CHANNEL.md` (for example "Which
   country should we do next? Drop the weirdest saying from YOUR language"). Pin it where the platform allows.
2. **Read everything new** on the last 3–5 episodes, including replies under your own comments (filters often hide them).
3. **Reply to each real comment once**: specific to what they said, short, warm, in the channel's tone and the commenter's
   language where possible. No copy-paste templates, no replies to yourself, no arguing, no spam. Hide or ignore abuse;
   do not feed it. If a commenter corrects a fact, check it — if they are right, thank them and note the correction.
4. **Requests** (a topic, country, question to cover):
   - already published → point to it (a link on YouTube; the title on platforms where links are not clickable);
   - planned but not produced → move it up to the next free production slot and tell them the real date;
   - not planned → if it fits the niche and guardrails, add it to `plan.csv` and tell them the date; otherwise reply
     kindly without promising.
   Record every request in `requests.csv`. Never promise a date you have not put in the plan, and do not move episodes
   that are already scheduled.
5. **Follow-up**: when a requested episode goes live, reply to the original comment ("It's live! … did we do it justice?")
   and mark it in `requests.csv`. Crediting the requester in the description is a nice touch.
6. **Listen**: note recurring questions, confusions and wishes in `insights.md` → Analysis log. Comments explain numbers.

If a platform refuses a comment ("couldn't post"), try once more later; new accounts hit spam limits and pushing makes it worse.

## Editing
The standing question, tone of replies, what counts as an acceptable request and the reply permission are in `CHANNEL.md`;
change them with `channel-edit`.
