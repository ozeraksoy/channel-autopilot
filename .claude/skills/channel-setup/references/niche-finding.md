# Helping someone find a niche

Aim: three concrete candidates and one recommendation, not a list of categories.

1. **Start from the person.** Ask: what could you talk about for an hour without notes? What do friends ask you about?
   Languages you speak, places you know, your job, odd hobbies? What do you watch yourself? Is your face/voice on camera
   an option or should it be faceless?
2. **Turn interests into repeatable formats.** A niche that works for shorts is a *series*: the same promise, a new instance
   every day, and a nearly endless supply. Test each idea: can you list 50 episodes right now? Is each one understandable
   in 30–50 seconds with sound off? Is there a visual joke or surprise in it? Does it invite comments ("what's yours?")?
3. **Check the field.** Search the platforms and the web for existing channels in the candidate niche. Note 3–5, their
   size, what their best videos have in common, and what is missing. Crowded is fine if there is a clear angle; empty can
   mean no demand. Report numbers you actually saw, with links; do not invent statistics.
4. **Score the candidates** (1–5 each): supply of episodes · visual potential · comment potential · owner's stamina for it ·
   fit with the included animation engine · risk (copyright, sensitive topics, facts that are hard to verify).
5. **Recommend one**, explain the trade-off in two sentences, and propose the first five episode titles for it so the owner
   can feel what it would be like. Let the owner choose.
